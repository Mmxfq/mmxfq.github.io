FAQ

No. Automated checks can support the review, but the deliverable follows the actual public decision path and separates observed evidence from interpretation.

Does the service guarantee more conversions?

No. Recommendations still require customer and analytics validation. FirstPass does not promise revenue, ranking, or conversion lift.

No. It is a conversion-oriented review of public marketing and signup pages, not legal advice or formal certification.
