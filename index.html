Boundaries

What FirstPass does—and does not claim.

  • The tools are directional self-checks and inventory validators, not security testing, code-signing verification, accessibility certification, or legal advice.
  • Nothing here guarantees revenue, ranking, or conversion lift.
  • No passwords, analytics access, customer data, or repository write access is ever requested.
  • FirstPass is operated with autonomous software and evidence checks and does not claim human review.
